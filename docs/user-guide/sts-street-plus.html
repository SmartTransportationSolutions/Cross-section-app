STS Street Plus

STS Street is free to use for everyone. STS Street Plus is a membership tier that unlocks additional design and export capabilities intended for professional work. It is activated by Smart Transportation Solutions (STS) on your account.

Features​

Plus members get:

  • Rename street elements — give any element a custom label (for example “Bus lane (peak hours)”).
  • Change environment backgrounds — time of day, fog and other scene presets from the Environment panel.
  • Export images without the watermark.
  • Export images at print-ready resolutions — custom scale up to 5× in the “Save as image” dialog.
  • Additional elements and variants — double-decker bus, microvan, autonomous shuttle, mixed-traffic drive lanes (with bus or bike) and the drainage channel.

All other capabilities — unlimited saved streets, galleries, sharing, remixing, standard image export, printing, the full element catalog and the 3DStreet / Streetmeter integrations — are available to every signed-in user.

How to get STS Street Plus​

  1. Sign in to STS Street (or create an account) so that membership can be linked to your username.
  2. Open Get STS Street Plus in the menu bar and choose Request membership by email, or write to info@sts.com.ge with your username.
  3. STS activates the membership on your account. Reload the editor; a Plus badge appears next to your avatar.

There is no self-service payment in STS Street at this time. Commercial terms, if any, are agreed directly with STS.

Confirming your membership​

When membership is active you will see the Plus badge beside your avatar in the menu bar, and the Settings → Profile panel lists your roles.

Troubleshooting​

  • Features still locked after activation: sign out and sign in again, or reload the page; membership is read when you sign in.
  • Export still shows the watermark: the watermark checkbox in “Save as image” only becomes editable for members; the server also enforces this, so a stale page will keep the watermark until it is reloaded.
  • Still stuck? Contact info@sts.com.ge.
