Privacy policy

This is the privacy policy for the STS Street edition published on GitHub Pages by Smart Transportation Solutions. It describes what this software does with data. Smart Transportation Solutions may replace this text with its reviewed legal wording; the technical statements below are accurate for this release.

Who operates this service

STS Street is operated by Smart Transportation Solutions, Tbilisi, Georgia. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@sts.com.ge.

How this edition works

This edition has no Smart Transportation Solutions server. The web page is hosted by GitHub Pages and runs entirely in your browser. Smart Transportation Solutions does not receive or store your street designs, your GitHub token or your settings.

Where your data is stored

  • Without signing in. Streets you create are stored only in your browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Signed in with GitHub. Each street is saved as a public GitHub Gist in your own GitHub account, so anyone with the link can view it, as on other street design tools. Your preferences are saved in a secret gist. GitHub stores this data under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Your GitHub token. The personal access token you paste to sign in is kept in your browser’s local storage and is sent only to the GitHub API. Signing out removes it from the browser. You can revoke it at any time in your GitHub settings.

Services your browser contacts

  • GitHub Pages (to load this site) and the GitHub API (to read and save gists).
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles, only when you open the “Location” dialog.
  • The “Open in 3DStreet” and “Open in Streetmeter” menu items send the public link of the current street to those third-party products only when you click them.

This edition contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and sends no data to the upstream Streetmix project.

Your choices

  • Delete a street from the “My streets” gallery, or delete the gist directly on GitHub.
  • “Delete account” in Settings deletes all STS Street gists from your GitHub account and signs you out.
  • Contact info@sts.com.ge with any privacy question.

Cookies

This edition sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for streets created without signing in, your preferences, and your GitHub sign-in.

STS Street is operated by Smart Transportation Solutions. Contact: info@sts.com.ge.
